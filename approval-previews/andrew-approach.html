What is Whey Protein?

Whey protein is a dairy-derived functional ingredient used where protein contribution, nutrition, texture and process performance are important. Final suitability depends on protein content, grade, flavour profile, solubility and the intended application.

Applications

Where it is used

  • Nutrition powders and protein beverages
  • Bakery mixes and protein-enriched products
  • Dairy formulations and dry blends
  • Bars, snacks and fortified foods
